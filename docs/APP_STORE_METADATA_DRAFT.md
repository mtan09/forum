## Description

forum: Every Side is a social news and political discussion app built to help
you understand how the same issue is framed across the political spectrum.

See news coverage and community posts in one feed. Move between For You,
Random, and Against You to follow your interests, encounter unfamiliar views,
and avoid getting trapped in a single perspective.

Explore the day's major stories through attributed Left, Center, and Right
headlines from multiple publishers. Open any publisher card to read the full
article at its original source. forum does not display copied article bodies.

Join the conversation:

- Create posts and threaded comments
- Follow people and manage private follow requests
- Vote, save, report, and block
- Send direct messages
- Place your position in The Floor and compare the community distribution
- Search publisher articles and community posts separately

forumAI offers optional Left, Center, and Right explanations grounded in
eligible attributed headlines from forum's indexed coverage. Before forum sends
personal data or user content to OpenAI, it explains what will be shared and
asks for permission. AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect.

Choose light or dark mode, control notification channels, reset feed
personalization, submit feedback, and delete your account from Settings.

forum has no advertising, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or cross-app
tracking.
